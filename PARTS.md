# Smart Puck parts list

Everything you need to build one cordless Smart Puck.

| Part | Qty | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| [1.28" ESP32-S3 round knob touch display](https://link.amazon/B03kXB6wJ) | 1 | ESP32-S3 with 8 MB PSRAM and 16 MB flash, 240×240 round touchscreen, knurled aluminum dial with push, 5 RGB LEDs. The puck's brain. Keep the 4-pin-to-USB cable it comes with for flashing. |
| [103450 LiPo battery, 3.7 V 2000 mAh](https://link.amazon/B02DtfgWK) | 1 | About 50 × 34 × 10 mm, with built-in protection. |
| [Type-C USB 5V 2A boost converter step-up power module](https://link.amazon/B01md2LDM) | 1 | Charges the battery over USB-C and boosts it to 5 V for the display. About 20.4 mm wide and 26.1 mm long; it slides into rails at the back of the tub. |
| [Black 2-pin snap-in mini rocker switch](https://link.amazon/B0iMg7XAl) | 1 | Front lip 20.76 × 12.6 mm, about 12 mm deep. Sits in the 5 V line. |
| [MX1.25 4-pin plug wires](https://link.amazon/B00jjsFpj) | 1 | Plugs into the display's "USB IN (5V)" socket. Only 5 V and GND are used. |
| [M3 × 16 socket-head screws](https://link.amazon/B0aCaM2bR) | 4 | Hold the lid to the tub. They thread into 2.6 mm pilot holes in the lid. |
| [Soldering iron with heat-set insert tool](https://link.amazon/B0j9AnaSR) | 1 | For wiring, and optionally for M3 heat-set inserts in the lid. |
| [Black PLA+](https://link.amazon/B0hBDP492) | 1 spool | Lid. A spool prints many pucks. |
| [Grey PLA+](https://link.amazon/B08KjhbuN) | 1 spool | Tub. |
| 10 mm round rubber feet | 4 | Optional. Fit the recesses under the tub. |

**Printer:** the reference build was printed on a [Bambu Lab A1 mini](https://link.amazon/B0hqkkMkx). Any printer with a bed of at least 100 × 70 mm works.

**Supplies:** rosin-core solder, heat-shrink or electrical tape, flush cutters, wire strippers, a 2.5 mm hex key, double-sided foam tape and a multimeter (strongly recommended, to check polarity before you plug in the display).
